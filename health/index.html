THE CHALLENGE

Care is connected. Operations should be, too.

Healthcare providers often coordinate referrals, eligibility, schedules, teams, service delivery, and outcomes across disconnected processes. MADAAR HEALTH explores a shared operational view that helps the right people see what needs attention.

01 / VISIBILITY

One service picture

Understand the status of requests, appointments, visits, and follow-up without relying on scattered updates.

02 / COORDINATION

Clear ownership

Make handoffs, responsibilities, and next actions easier to understand across clinical and operations teams.

03 / IMPROVEMENT

Measure service performance

Explore operational indicators that help leaders identify delays, workload pressure, and improvement opportunities.

PRODUCT SCOPE

An operational toolkit for the care journey.

These are proposed product modules for discovery and validation — not features currently available for clinical use.

MODULE 01

Referral & intake

Capture incoming service requests, required information, referral source, and current status.

MODULE 02

Eligibility & authorization

Organize coverage checks, approvals, missing requirements, and clear escalation ownership.

MODULE 03

Scheduling & capacity

Explore team availability, service zones, appointment coordination, and workload distribution.

MODULE 04

Care journey tracking

Follow operational milestones from accepted request through service delivery and follow-up.

MODULE 05

Outcomes & quality

Support structured capture of service outcomes and quality indicators defined by the provider.

MODULE 06

Management insights

Bring operational trends, service volumes, bottlenecks, and team capacity into management views.

SAFETY & PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

Operational support. Clinical decisions stay with clinicians.

Intended direction

  • Operational coordination and workflow visibility.
  • Role-based access, traceable actions, and accountable ownership.
  • Provider-defined quality and service indicators.

Not represented as available

  • Diagnosis, prescribing, or autonomous treatment recommendations.
  • Emergency monitoring or replacement of clinical judgment.
  • A certified medical device or a live patient-record system.

MADAAR HEALTH is at concept stage. Any future handling of health or patient information will require privacy-by-design, access controls, auditability, security testing, and applicable Saudi regulatory and healthcare-sector review before deployment.
