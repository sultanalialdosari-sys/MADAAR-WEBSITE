THE CHALLENGE

The signal is buried in the noise.

Teams make decisions while information is scattered across announcements, reports, public records, competitor activity, and industry sources. MADAAR INTEL explores a disciplined research workspace that connects evidence to opportunities — and keeps the source trail visible.

01 / DISCOVER

PRODUCT SCOPE

A research system, not another news feed.

Proposed modules for a source-aware intelligence workflow. Coverage, integrations, and automation will be defined only after validation.

MODULE 01

Market radar

Group relevant public signals by sector, geography, topic, and time.

MODULE 02

Competitor profiles

Track public positioning, product changes, partnerships, hiring, and announcements with source links.

MODULE 03

Opportunity workspace

Compare opportunities using transparent criteria such as demand evidence, timing, competition, and execution effort.

MODULE 04

Research library

Keep source documents, notes, findings, and research history organized and searchable.

MODULE 05

Evidence-linked briefs

Produce structured summaries that distinguish verified facts, interpretation, and open questions.

MODULE 06

Signal monitoring

Explore saved topics and change alerts when reliable source access and update frequency are established.

EVIDENCE & RESPONSIBLE USE

Every insight should show its working.

Product principles

  • Traceable sources and visible publication dates.
  • Separate sourced facts from analysis and hypotheses.
  • Transparent comparison criteria and uncertainty.

What is not claimed

  • Complete coverage of every market or competitor.
  • Guaranteed forecasts, returns, or investment outcomes.
  • Live monitoring or real-time data before integrations exist.

MADAAR INTEL is at concept stage. Any future data collection will use public or properly authorized sources, respect applicable terms and privacy requirements, and make source dates and limitations visible.
